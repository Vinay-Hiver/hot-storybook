# Slack

The conversation view for the Slack channel. It does not look like Chat, SMS and WhatsApp: messages are full-width rows with an avatar, a name and a time, not bubbles.

- **Top bar**: the customer's name, and the Slack channel they are in as a chip (for example `#support`). 44px tall, light grey.
- **Thread**: the messages, oldest first. The agent is shown as "Aarav Mehta (You)". Pointing at a message shows a toolbar to add a reaction, delete your own message and open it in Slack. Reactions show under the message, and pressing one removes it.
- **Reply box**: the same box as the other channels, with a hint about Slack templates. Send turns on as soon as there is text, and Enter sends.

## The parts

- **Message row**: a 32px avatar, the name and the time (in light grey), then the text. Pointing at a row turns it light grey and shows a toolbar at its top right: add a reaction (10 emoji to pick from), delete (agent messages only) and open in Slack. Reactions are small grey pills with a count, and pressing one removes it.

In Storybook, a generic reply from the customer arrives about 2 seconds after the agent sends a message, to mimic a conversation.
