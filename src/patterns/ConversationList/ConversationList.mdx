# Conversation list

The column of conversation cards that opens when a view of a shared inbox is chosen in the Conversations sidebar, for example Chat → Unassigned. It is 300px wide and scrolls on its own.

- **Header**: an icon and the name of the view.
- **Select all:** under it, a checkbox selects every conversation shown in the view (a dash means only some are selected). Each card's own checkbox, which replaces its channel icon on hover, stays in step with it.
- **Status tabs (tag view only):** when the list is filtered by a tag, a row of tabs sits right under the title: **Unresolved** (open and pending, with a count and a small arrow), **Closed** and **All**. The arrow opens a small menu with checkboxes for Open and Pending (both ticked) to choose what Unresolved covers; the count follows. A conversation's status is `open` unless the data says `pending` or `closed`. Going to another view hides the tabs.
- **Tab widths and menu:** the three tabs fill the width (each at least as wide as its own content, so "Unresolved 1 ⌄" is never cut off) and the active underline sits on the bottom edge. In this view the "All Conversations" label has no dropdown (the tabs do the filtering). The Open / Pending menu opens right under the Unresolved tab, lined up with its left edge.
- **Empty:** when nothing is shown, a grey band says how many ("0 Closed conversations", "0 Unresolved conversations", "0 conversations") and "No conversations found" sits in the middle of the list. On the Home page the area beside the list also shows the Empty state (envelope icon, "No conversations found", "Take a chill pill!") instead of a conversation and the right panel.
- **All Conversations:** next to the checkbox, with a small arrow. Pressing it opens a menu with **Show all** (ticked) and **Unread only**; choosing Unread only keeps just the unread conversations and the label becomes "Unread Conversations". Going to another view clears the selection.
- **Cards**: one Conversation card per conversation, with the channel icon of the inbox. Pressing a card selects it. Opening the conversation is not built yet.

Tags and All Views do not show a list.

The icon before the title can be a button (`onIconClick`). On the Home page it hides the Conversations sidebar, so the list sits next to the Main nav, and shows it again on the next press.

Each card's second line is the latest message of the conversation: the first line of a message that was received, or a message that was sent with the reply arrow. Never an activity such as "chat was reopened".

Each channel has its own dummy conversations (5 to 8). Every view of that channel shows the same list for now, and the counts in the sidebar match.
