# Conversation list

The column of conversation cards that opens when a view of a shared inbox is chosen in the Conversations sidebar, for example Chat → Unassigned. It is 300px wide and scrolls on its own.

- **Header**: an icon and the name of the view.
- **All Conversations**: a small heading under it.
- **Cards**: one Conversation card per conversation, with the channel icon of the inbox. Pressing a card selects it. Opening the conversation is not built yet.

Tags and All Views do not show a list.

Each channel has its own dummy conversations (5 to 8). Every view of that channel shows the same list for now, and the counts in the sidebar match.
