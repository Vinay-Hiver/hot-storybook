# SMS

The conversation view for the SMS channel. The layout is the same as Chat, with one difference.

- **Agent messages** are a white card with a thin border, as on WhatsApp, not a blue bubble. They have no ticks.

Messages from the customer are light grey on the left, the reply box is the same as in Chat, and messages are 12px apart.

In Storybook, a generic reply from the customer arrives about 2 seconds after the agent sends a message, to mimic a conversation.
