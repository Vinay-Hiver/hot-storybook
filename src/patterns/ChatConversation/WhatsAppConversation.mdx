# WhatsApp

The conversation view for the WhatsApp channel. The layout is the same as Chat, with three differences.

- **Agent messages** are a white card with a thin border, not a blue bubble.
- **Ticks** after the agent's name and time show how far the message has got: one grey tick (sent), two grey ticks (delivered) or two blue ticks (read).
- **Reply box** has a third button for WhatsApp templates, and its hint mentions them.

Messages from the customer are light grey on the left, and messages are 12px apart.

## The parts

- **Messages**: the agent's message is a white card with a thin border, and the customer's is light grey. Under the agent's message, ticks show how far it has got: one grey tick (sent), two grey ticks (delivered) or two blue ticks (read). Bubbles hug their text and are at most 450px wide.
- **Reply box**: the same as in Chat, with a third button that opens the WhatsApp templates (it does nothing yet). The hint mentions templates.

In Storybook, a generic reply from the customer arrives about 2 seconds after the agent sends a message, to mimic a conversation.
