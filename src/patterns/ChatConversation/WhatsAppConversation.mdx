# WhatsApp

The conversation view for the WhatsApp channel. The layout is the same as Chat, with three differences.

- **Agent messages** are a white card with a thin border, not a blue bubble.
- **Ticks** after the agent's name and time show how far the message has got: one grey tick (sent), two grey ticks (delivered) or two blue ticks (read).
- **Reply box** has a third button for WhatsApp templates, and its hint mentions them.

Messages from the customer are pale blue on the left, as in Chat, and messages are 12px apart.
