# Right panel

The panel on the right of a page, always 320px wide. Every channel uses the same anatomy and only the content changes: a **top section** followed by **sub-sections** that open and close. There are four sub-sections here for now.

## Anatomy

At the very top there is a bar with a tab (the Shared Inbox tab, an icon with a 2px underline when selected). The panel's content starts below it, and only the content scrolls. The top section has a heading (the name of the inbox the conversation belongs to) and a few fields, such as Assignee, Status and Tags. The Tags field shows the conversation's tags, each with a small x to remove it, and an **Add** chip that opens a searchable list of every tag with checkboxes: ticking and unticking only takes effect when you press **Apply** (Cancel or a click outside keeps the tags as they were); the chips wrap onto more lines when there are many. Only the Email channel also has a Co-assignee under Assignee, which starts as None. Under it, each sub-section has an icon, a name and an arrow. Inside a sub-section, each row is an icon and a label on the left, some space, and the value on the right. The rows are plain markup built from the Icon and text with colour tokens, not a separate component: the icon and label use the subtle text colour and the value uses the title text colour (or the link colour for a number you can click).

## Open and closed

A closed sub-section shows only its header with an arrow pointing right. An open one shows its fields and the arrow points down. Clicking the header switches between the two, sliding over 250ms with an ease-in-out curve.

## Reordering

Drag a sub-section by its handle (the dots that show on the left when you point at it) to move it up or down. The section follows the pointer folded up, on a white card with a soft shadow, and the others slide out of the way. When you let go it settles into place and opens again if it was open. Clicking the rest of the header still opens or closes it. The top section stays at the top.

## Customize widgets

A "Customize widgets" button sits after the last section. Clicking it opens a small list above the button with the name of every section and a checkbox. Only the ticked sections show in the panel, so unticking Account removes it, and ticking it again brings it back in the same place. The list stays exactly where it opened while you tick and untick sections, even though the button moves up when sections are hidden; it only takes a new place the next time you open it. It closes when you click outside it or press Escape. The names can also be dragged into a new order (point at a row and use the handle on its right), and the sections in the panel follow. All sections are ticked at first.

## Hover

Pointing at a sub-section, open or closed, turns it light grey and shows a drag handle on the left, because the sections can be dragged to a new order from the handle. The closed Account section and the open Contact section below are pinned to show it.

## Editable custom fields

The Custom fields section can hold fields the person edits right there. There are three kinds:

- **Text**: press the value to turn it into a text box. Enter or leaving the box saves it, Escape drops the change.
- **Number**: the same, but it only takes digits and one decimal point.
- **Date**: pressing the value opens a calendar under it (the react-day-picker library, in our colours). Picking a day saves it, shown like "Nov 18, 2026", and picking the same day again clears it.

An empty field shows "Empty" in the disabled text colour. In code, use `CustomFieldsList` (it keeps its own values) or a single `CustomField`.
