# Main nav

The thin dark bar down the far left of the app. It is 44px wide and fills the height of its container. It has the Hiver logo, a column of icons, and at the bottom a couple of small icons and the person's avatar.

## Default

Give it `items` (each with an `id`, a `label` and an `icon`). Set `value` to the `id` of the selected one and listen to `onChange` to switch areas. The selected icon sits on a lighter slate square. Pointing at an icon shows its `label` in a tooltip. `footerItems` are the small icons above the avatar, and `user` sets the avatar's letter and online status.
