# Conversations sidebar

The menu on the left of the inbox that lists where conversations live. It is 240px wide and fills the height of its container. It has a heading with a search button and a plus button, then groups of rows: My Work and Personal, the shared inboxes, and more folders such as Sent and Drafts.

## Default

Each row has an icon, a name and, for inboxes, an unread count.

<Canvas of={SidebarStories.Default} />

## Selected and hover

The selected row has a light blue background and blue text. Pointing at a row turns it light grey. Slack is selected below and Email is pinned to show hover.
