# Tooltip

A short label that appears when someone hovers or focuses an element, to explain what it does. Keep it to a few words. Do not put anything the person must read in a tooltip, because it is not shown on touch screens.

## Placement

The tooltip can appear on any side of the element. It shows on hover and on keyboard focus.
