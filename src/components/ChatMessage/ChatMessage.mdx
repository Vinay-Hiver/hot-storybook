# Chat message

One message in a Chat conversation. It has two states, depending on who sent it.

- **User**: the customer. A pale blue bubble on the left, with the time under it.
- **Agent**: someone on the team. A solid blue bubble with white text on the right, with the agent's name and the time under it.

A message is at most 450px wide, and longer text wraps.

## WhatsApp

On WhatsApp the agent's message is a white card with a thin border instead of a blue bubble, and it shows ticks after the time: one grey tick when it is sent, two grey ticks when it is delivered and two blue ticks when it has been read. Chat messages have no ticks.
