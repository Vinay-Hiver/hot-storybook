# Avatar

A round badge with a person's first letter and a small dot that shows whether they are online. It is also used inside **List item** and **Dropdown**.

## Sizes

Default is 24px and small is 18px.

<Canvas of={AvatarStories.Sizes} />

## Status

A green dot means online and a grey dot means offline. The dot is always shown.
