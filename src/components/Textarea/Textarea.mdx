# Textarea

A multi-line text field, 128px tall by default and resizable vertically. It shares its label, helper text and states with **Input**.

## Tags

Add `tags` (or `defaultTags`) to turn the field into a tags field. Typing and pressing Enter or comma adds a **Tag**, and the tags wrap onto more lines as they grow.
